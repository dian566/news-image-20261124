## What it is

A single 1024×1024 PNG (`entry.png`, 430,711 bytes) in newspaper front-page
style, titled "WORLD NEWS — 24 NOVEMBER 2026", summarising the six biggest
world stories of 24 November 2026:

1. **NATO sends AWACS jets to Poland** after three Russian Kh-101 missiles
   landed in the country.
2. **Ukraine sinks a Russian warship in Libya** (corvette Merkury, port of
   Benghazi) — the war spreading beyond Europe; a Russian cargo ship was
   also hit in the Gulf of Finland.
3. **COP31 climate talks collapse in Turkey** (Antalya), triggering backlash
   and despair among Pacific nations.
4. **US–Iran standoff**: Iran vows to confront the US naval blockade; the US
   imposes new sanctions on Iran-linked shadow banking networks.
5. **Factory fire in Indonesia kills 24** workers at a candy factory in
   Banten province.
6. **BepiColombo probe enters orbit around Mercury** (ESA/JAXA mission).

Sources: Reuters world news summary, 24 Nov 2026; Times of India / Jagran
Josh school-assembly headlines for 24 Nov 2026.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the image to `/output/entry.png` (byte-for-byte identical to
`entry.png` in this repo; verified locally, deterministic across runs).
