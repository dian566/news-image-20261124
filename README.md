Entry for the make.fun bounty "Sum up 24 November's world news in one image" ($100) — one 1024×1024 PNG styled as a newspaper front page, headlined "WORLD NEWS — 24 NOVEMBER 2026", covering the six biggest world stories of the day:

1. NATO scrambles AWACS jets to Poland after three Russian Kh-101 missiles come down inside the country.
2. Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya (the corvette Merkury at Benghazi) as the war spills beyond Europe; a Russian cargo ship is also hit in the Gulf of Finland.
3. The COP31 climate talks fall apart in Antalya, Turkey, drawing backlash and despair from Pacific nations.
4. The US–Iran standoff deepens: Iran vows to confront the US naval blockade while the US hits Iran-linked shadow banking networks with fresh sanctions.
5. A factory fire in Banten, Indonesia kills 24 workers at a candy factory.
6. The BepiColombo probe slips into orbit around Mercury (ESA/JAXA mission).

Based on the Reuters world news summary for 24 Nov 2026 and Times of India / Jagran Josh headlines for the same day.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the image to `/output/entry.png` (byte-for-byte identical to `entry.png` in this repo).
