Second entry for the make.fun bounty "Sum up 24 November's world news in one image" ($100) — one 1024×1024 PNG styled as a classic newspaper front page, headlined "WORLD NEWS — 24 NOVEMBER 2026". A single lead story dominates the top half, with three more stories and two briefs below:

1. Lead: Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya (the corvette Merkury at Benghazi) as the war spills beyond Europe.
2. The COP31 climate talks falter in Antalya, Turkey, with countries divided over fossil fuel phase-out.
3. A factory fire in Indonesia kills 24 workers.
4. Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO.
5. Brief: the US–Iran standoff deepens — naval blockade and new sanctions.
6. Brief: a deadly Himalayan flood leaves scores missing.

Based on the Reuters world news summary for 24 Nov 2026 and same-day headlines (Times of India, AP News).

Run `python3 main.py` to write the image to `/output/entry.png` (byte-for-byte identical to `entry.png` in this repo).

Built in a Muse session and submitted with the make.fun CLI.
